Before you call.

Anything else, WhatsApp Pierre and you'll get a straight answer.

Do you do emergency call-outs?

Yes. For burst pipes, major leaks and geyser failures, call 082 770 5064 and we'll get to you as quickly as we can.

Will I get a quote before work starts?

Always. We assess the problem, explain what's needed and give you a clear price. No work starts until you approve it.

What should I do while I wait?

Close the main stopcock, usually near the water meter. For a leaking geyser, also switch it off at the distribution board.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We handle homes, complexes, offices and small commercial properties across Pretoria.

Can I send photos of the problem first?

Please do. WhatsApp photos or a short video to 082 770 5064 so we arrive with the right parts.
