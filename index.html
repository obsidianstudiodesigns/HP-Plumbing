FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Do you do emergency call-outs?

Yes. For burst pipes, major leaks and geyser failures, call Pierre directly on 082 770 5064 and we'll get to you as quickly as possible.

Will I get a quote before work starts?

Always. We assess the problem, explain what's needed and give you a clear price. No work starts until you approve it.

What should I do while I wait for the plumber?

Turn off your main water supply at the stopcock (usually near the water meter). For geyser leaks, also switch off the geyser at the distribution board.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes — we handle residential homes, complexes, offices and small commercial properties across Pretoria.

Can I send photos of the problem first?

Please do! WhatsApp photos or a short video to 082 770 5064 — it helps us come prepared with the right parts.
